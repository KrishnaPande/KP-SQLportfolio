Deliver end-to-end consulting and analytics solutions for retail clients across:
1. Merchandise Planning

2. Supply Chain Planning

3. Demand Forecasting & Replenishment

4. Store Operations

5. Space Planning

Conduct deep-dive analysis of retail business processes, operational data, and financial metrics to identify gaps, risks, and improvement opportunities
Develop actionable insights, transformation roadmaps, and data-driven recommendations
Collaborate closely with client stakeholders and internal cross-functional teams to co-create and implement solutions
Lead and support change management initiatives to ensure solution adoption and measurable business impact
Manage multiple workstreams in fast-paced, high-pressure environments while maintaining quality and timelines
