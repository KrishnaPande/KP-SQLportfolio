Job Description: Global Analyst

In this role, you will be a core member of the advisory team, supporting high-priority strategic projects. You will go beyond simple data analysis to generate the insights, storylines, and recommendations that drive client decisions.


In this role you will:

Lead deep-dive market and competitive research for niche segments, conducting market sizing, value chain analysis, and trend analysis to inform client go-to-market and expansion strategies.
Develop and deliver executive-level presentations, reports, and interactive dashboards (e.g., Power BI), translating complex data into a clear and compelling storyline.
Support strategy, M&A, and procurement projects by delivering financial, macroeconomic, and supply-chain diagnostics.
Engage directly with internal stakeholders and clients to support Commercial Due Diligence (CDD), portfolio reviews, and cost optimization exercises.
Analyze financial and operational data to conduct peer benchmarking and identify performance gaps.
Manage project workstreams, track milestones, and demonstrate initiative in addressing client and team challenges.
Support the development of structured knowledge assets, such as competitive benchmarks and strategic playbooks.


WHAT YOU’LL BRING

Minimum Qualifications

Bachelor’s degree from an accredited institution.
Three (3) or more years of professional experience in strategy consulting, competitive intelligence, or corporate strategy, preferably from a global research or top-tier consulting firm.
Strong analytical skills, with proven experience in hypothesis generation, financial analysis, and peer benchmarking.
Excellent written and verbal communication skills, with the ability to build a "storyline" and present findings clearly to executive audiences.
Demonstrated ability to manage multiple priorities in a fast-paced environment, delivering client-ready output on quick turnarounds.
Proficiency with Microsoft Office (Excel, PowerPoint).


Technical Requirements & Competencies

Advanced Data Proficiency: We are looking for candidates with an Advanced Excel toolkit (beyond basic formulas). Exposure to coding or a deep understanding of data/analytics architecture is a strong advantage.
Technical Aptitude: While prior professional coding experience is not a prerequisite, you must demonstrate clear technical instincts and a proven ability to master new data tools rapidly.
The Hybrid Mindset: This role requires a "Hybrid Consultant" approach—someone who is equally comfortable in client-facing delivery as they are performing deep-dive technical analysis.


Experience & Background

Professional Standing: A minimum of 2–3 years of professional experience is required.
Functional Hybridity: We prefer candidates who have a background blending business consulting with hands-on data analysis. You should be able to translate complex data into actionable strategic insights for clients.


Preferred Qualifications

A Master's degree (PGPM, MBA, or similar) with a focus on Finance, Strategy, or Business.
Hands-on experience with financial, market, and trade databases such as S&P Capital IQ, Pitchbook, Euromonitor, TradeMap, or Thomson Reuters.
Advanced proficiency in data analysis and visualization tools, including Power BI, and experience with social media analytics tools (e.g., BrandWatch).
Experience in Consumer Products & Retail (CPR) or Advanced Manufacturing/Mobility sectors.
Prior experience supporting procurement or strategic sourcing projects, including vendor identification and profiling.
